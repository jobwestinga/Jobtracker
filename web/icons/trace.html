Add this icon to your Home Screen.

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown. Name it JobTracker and tap Add.
  4. Open the new icon. Reconnect with your existing device token if asked, then check that your history is present.

First let pending changes in your current app sync. Keep the old icon until the new one works. Do not clear website data or remove an app with unsent changes.

iOS may keep the old icon until you add a new Home Screen item. Changing this page does not change existing icons automatically.
