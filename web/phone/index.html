  1. Keep Tailscale connected when reaching your private server. Phone modes and Shortcuts work independently.
  2. Open each new Home Screen app and enter its access token. Each installation may have separate storage, so sign in to both. Ask your server administrator for a new device token if needed; tokens never belong in links.
  3. Tap Refresh. Check that the status says Synced, with no changes waiting. Compare your history with the Mac app. Offline saves say they are waiting and send after reconnection while the app is open.

Removing a Home Screen app deletes its token, unfinished forms and any changes still waiting to send. Let each app say Synced first; a server backup cannot protect unsent phone changes.

A Safari link does not reliably open another installed app. Launch the intended Home Screen icon. Installation prompts, icon changes and offline relaunch must be checked on your iPhone.
